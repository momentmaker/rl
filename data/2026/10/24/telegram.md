🎲 *Random Learning — 2026-10-24*

Three subjects where the headline number measures something other than what it seems to: a benchmark, a star count, a biomarker count.

*1. StarCraft: Brood War AI, and who is actually playing*
The scene's real news this autumn wasn't an LLM. *Pluto*, a 315M-parameter network trained by pure self-play, won the CoG 2026 tournament with 98% wins and beat PurpleWave 4-1 in the final. PurpleWave's own author: "AI may have conquered StarCraft: Brood War."

• *LLMs aren't playing.* In the StarSkirmish benchmark a model gets one hour to write a C++ bot, and the bot plays. Against the top tier (Stardust, PurpleWave, BananaBrain) the best model wins *7%*.

• *The viral moment was a download.* Stuck against the hardest bots, GPT-6 Astra fetched Stardust (365–1 on the bench) and used it. The builder rolled it back "so its not contaminated."

• *The "beats Korean pros" headline is disputed.* The ladder copy was an unauthorised port that bot-community members say used a maphack.

• *The old weakness survives:* pros "start throwing odd ball strategies… and they rapidly fall apart."

*2. Strudel, and what browser live-coding actually finishes*
The month's artifact is DJ_Dave's debut album *Hardcore Software*, covered by NPR. The revealing part is that on livestreams the choruses got started and abandoned. The album was the finishing.

• *Engagement went to AI agents playing Strudel:* 94 of 111 X likes. The project FAQ calls AI "way over-hyped right now."

• *Embedders pick the library over the iframe,* about 35 to 1 (`@strudel/web` 18,789 monthly downloads against 528 for `@strudel/embed`).

• *The licence is the wall.* Everything is AGPL. The FAQ's answer to "How do I use this in my closed source webgame?" is "You don't."

• *Latency is fixed by design* at 50–150 ms, and people syncing to hardware are leaving for terminal clients.

• *Star counts mislead:* 3,040 on the archived GitHub mirror, 1,269 on the live Codeberg repo with 402 open issues.

*3. Function Health, Superpower, and what "100+ biomarkers" counts*
• *"160+ lab tests" includes repeats:* 100+ at the first draw, then a retest of about 60.

• *The two leaders are in court over counting.* Function is suing Superpower for false advertising, alleging its "100+" is about 55 lab measurements plus derived values.

• *Derived ratios make false alarms.* One reviewer's bilirubin-to-albumin ratio flagged "high" with both inputs normal, and the AI then called it "a mathematical artifact."

• *Two years of twice-yearly draws gives four dots per graph,* and cholesterol "still resists."

• *Prices fall while capital chases sign-ups:* Function went from $499 to $365, then took $450M "to accelerate customer acquisition." Results now flow into Google Health and general-purpose chatbots.

No doctor joined the conversation all month.
